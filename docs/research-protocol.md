## Research Objectives
[REQUIRES RESEARCHER/SUPERVISOR APPROVAL]

This study aims to investigate:
1. The effect of anonymity on cyberbullying perpetration
2. The relationship between anonymity and moral disengagement
3. The role of empathy in cyberbullying behavior
4. The influence of pessimistic thinking on online aggression
